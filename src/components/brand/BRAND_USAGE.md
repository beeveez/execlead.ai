# EXECLEAD.AI Canonical Brand Usage

## Source of truth
Use `CanonicalBrandLockup` for every digital logo presentation and `ExecleadWordmark` for brand names inside body copy. Brand colors live only in `src/index.css`, are mapped in `tailwind.config.js`, and are mirrored in `BrandRegistry.colors`.

## Approved lockups
- `horizontal`: full E-mark, separator, and EXECLEAD.AI wordmark for desktop and tablet shells.
- `stacked`: centered mark and full wordmark for vertically composed brand moments.
- `wordmark`: complete EXECLEAD.AI wordmark when the mark is already present nearby.
- `icon`: E-mark only for compact identity surfaces and app-icon contexts.

Approved sizes are `sm`, `md`, `lg`, and `hero`; approved themes are `dark` and `light`.

## Rules
- Preserve the SVG aspect ratio and built-in clear space.
- Keep `.AI` visible and at the same proportional type size as `EXECLEAD`.
- Use only EXEC Gold, EXEC Silver, EXEC Blue, AI Blue, and EXEC Navy tokens.
- Use the full horizontal lockup on desktop and controlled proportional scaling on smaller screens.
- Use the icon variant only when the full lockup genuinely cannot fit.

## Never
Do not manually recreate the mark or wordmark, change spacing or typography, substitute colors, stretch or compress the SVG, separate `EXECLEAD` from `.AI`, shrink `.AI`, add shadows or outlines, or create page-specific logo variants.
